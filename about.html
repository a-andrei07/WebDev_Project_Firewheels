Welcome to our about page.

We here at Wheels on Fire have been established since 1996 and are going strong. It all began with John O'Reilly's passion for cars.

John: "Ever since I got my first car I dreamed to host my own car shows and have a collection of cars ranging from the oldest of the oldest to the most cutting edge of cars. Now that isn't a dream it is a reality. It all kicked off in secondary school when I got my very first car, bought for me by my dad as my 18th birthday gift. It was a chevrolet corvette coupe from the 1990s. It was rather fast and new at the time and it's what got me into cars. Since then I've been expanding my collection and I still have my first car that my dad bought me. Now I hope to teach others about cars and get them intrested in cars aswell. It's a whole new intresting world waiting for you out there"
